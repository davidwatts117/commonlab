Ohm's Law

V = IR

Voltage (V) equals current (I) times resistance (R). It is the relationship behind every circuit you build here.

Series Circuits

Req = R1 + R2 + …

Parts in a single loop share one current. Resistances add up, and the voltage splits between the parts.

Parallel Circuits

1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + …

Parts side by side share the same voltage. The current splits between the branches and Req is smaller than any branch.

Power

P = IV = I²R

Energy per second. A bulb lights up in proportion to the power it dissipates, so a brighter bulb means more current.

How To Use

Drag a part from the toolbox onto the board, or pick a tool and drag on the board to draw the part. Join two parts by dragging one end dot onto the other — a grey dot appears where they connect. Click a part to select it, then type an exact voltage or resistance in the box below the board (or double-click the part as a shortcut). Right-click, or use Erase, to remove a part.
