Ohm's Law

V = IR

Voltage (V) equals current (I) times resistance (R). The fundamental relationship governing all circuits.

Series Circuits

Req = R1 + R2 + ...

In series, resistances add directly. The same current flows through every component. Voltage divides across resistors.

Parallel Circuits

1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...

In parallel, the reciprocal of Req is the sum of reciprocals. Voltage is the same across each branch; current divides.

Power

P = IV = I²R = V²/R

Power dissipated as heat (or light in a bulb). Measured in watts (W). Higher current means more power.
